1. Information We Gather

We only gather data strictly required to sustain stable operation of reward‑focused gameplay, run anti‑fraud checks, and deliver optimized user experience. We avoid collecting unnecessary private information without your explicit permission. Data categories are listed below:

•                      Account & Profile Data: When you log‑in using authorized third‑party services such as Apple ID or Google Sign‑In, we obtain your registered email address, public display name and profile avatar. This information is used exclusively for account creation, identity validation, reward record tracking, and cross‑device synchronization of your gameplay and bonus balances.

•                      Device & Network Metadata: We collect advertising identifiers (IDFA for iOS, GAID for Android), public IP address, device model, operating system version, screen configuration, system language, time zone setting and active network type (Wi‑Fi or cellular). These technical details help us adapt the App for varied hardware setups, diagnose network failures, and carry out anti‑fraud risk assessments for reward claims.

•                      Game & Reward Interaction Records: We log in‑app behaviors including challenge participation status, task completion records, bonus redemption attempts, reward‑ad interactions, session duration, and feature usage frequency. Such anonymous records support reward mechanism tuning, risk monitoring, and gameplay experience refinement.

•                      System Diagnostic Logs: If the App encounters crashes, freezes or loading malfunctions, we automatically collect runtime logs, error codes and device performance snapshots. These logs serve only to locate technical defects and improve overall application stability. They do not carry personally identifiable markers.

•                      Optional Installed‑App Permission Request: Upon initial launch, integrated advertising SDKs may ask for permission to view your installed application list. You have full authority to deny this prompt. Denial will not block core game or reward‑claim functions. Our development team cannot access or store this list; all related processing is performed solely by the corresponding ad service provider under their own privacy rules.

2. Purposes of Using Your Information

All collected data shall only be processed for legitimate service‑related goals shown below. We will never repurpose your information for unrelated commercial activities unless you provide separate clear consent:

•                      Maintain core gameplay, synchronize challenge archives, track task completion status, and preserve your in‑app reward records across linked devices.

•                      Improve App responsiveness, resolve compatibility issues across different mobile devices, and fix software defects reported through diagnostic logs.

•                      Serve appropriate in‑app advertisements and reward‑earning tasks with support from device advertising identifiers.

•                      Process your support submissions, respond to reward‑related complaints, technical fault reports and general inquiries.

•                      Send in‑app notifications for new challenge events, bonus opportunities, service maintenance alerts and policy revisions.

•                      Detect fake accounts, duplicate reward claims, abuse of bonus rules and other suspicious behaviors to uphold fair reward rules for all participants.

3. Information Sharing Rules

We firmly will not sell, rent or trade your personal identifiable information for third‑party marketing purposes. Information may only be shared under these limited, compliance‑aligned scenarios:

•                      Your advertising identifier may be shared with certified ad partners to enable ad rendering, exposure statistics and rewarded‑task delivery. All cooperating partners sign binding confidentiality agreements and are prohibited from re‑using this identifier for outside business purposes.

•                      Aggregated, de‑identified statistical data that cannot trace back to individual users may be shared with analytical third parties for product optimization.

•                      Trusted service vendors including cloud storage operators and crash‑reporting tools may access limited operational data needed for their assigned duties. They must adhere to our defined privacy protection standards.

•                      We may disclose minimal necessary information in response to valid legal demands such as court subpoenas, official regulatory investigations or statutory obligations.

4. Data Security Protection

We implement multi‑layer technical and administrative safeguards to defend user data against unauthorized access, alteration, leakage and loss:

•                      All data transmitted between your mobile device and our backend servers is encrypted via TLS protocol. Persistently stored personal account data is protected with high‑strength encryption measures.

•                      Granular access controls are enforced: only authorized staff with valid business needs may access sensitive user information, and all relevant personnel are bound by formal non‑disclosure commitments.

•                      We perform periodic security audits, vulnerability scanning and system upgrades to counter evolving cybersecurity threats.

Please keep in mind that no electronic transmission or storage technology can guarantee absolute security. We take commercially reasonable steps to secure your information, yet complete safety cannot be guaranteed over internet‑based data transfers.

5. Data Retention Period

We retain user data only for the minimal time required to fulfill collection objectives or satisfy legal requirements:

•                      Your account details, reward records and gameplay archives are kept while your account stays active. After your valid deletion request is approved, these personally identifiable datasets get erased immediately.

•                      Anonymous gameplay metrics and system diagnostic logs are retained for at most 9 months; they will be anonymized or fully deleted upon expiry.

•                      Certain non‑identifiable anti‑fraud markers may be kept for a limited period to prevent reward abuse, and erased once risk‑monitoring work concludes.

•                      Data that must be preserved for legal compliance will be retained until statutory retention obligations expire.
